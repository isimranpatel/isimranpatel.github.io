Cardiovascular Heart Disease Analysis

Comparing eight ML classifiers to predict cardiovascular disease risk from patient health data

What I Did

Built a full machine learning pipeline, with two teammates, to analyze and predict cardiovascular disease risk from patient health data. I worked on data preprocessing — cleaning, handling null values, and treating outliers using the IQR method — then ran feature selection with correlation matrices, chi-squared testing, and Random Forest importance. We trained and compared eight classification models (KNN, Gaussian Naive Bayes, Decision Trees, SVM, AdaBoost, Random Forest, Logistic Regression, and an Artificial Neural Network), tuning them with GridSearchCV to identify the best-performing approach.

Why I Did It

I wanted hands-on practice turning a raw, messy healthcare dataset into a rigorous ML evaluation — not just fitting one model, but systematically comparing many side-by-side to understand which preprocessing and modeling choices actually mattered. That exploratory-analysis-to-model-comparison workflow is exactly what shows up in real data analysis work.
