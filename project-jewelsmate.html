Jewels Mate

Currently Working

A full-stack inventory, sales & accounting platform for a jewelry & diamond retailer, replacing manual/spreadsheet tracking with one system covering stock, vendors, customers, and transactions end-to-end

What I'm Building

Jewels Mate is a custom web application built for a jewelry and diamond retail business. It manages the full lifecycle of inventory — from receiving stock from vendors, to tracking it on the shelf, to selling it to customers — along with the accounting and reporting that goes with it. Access is role-based, so staff, admins, and customers each get a tailored view of the system.

Inventory Management

Styles, SKUs, live stock levels, a price calculator, and bulk stock import from Excel.

Vendor Workflows

Receiving goods via memo or invoice, plus vendor management.

Customer Workflows

Outgoing goods via memo, invoice, or sales order, plus customer management.

Accounting Module

Due/closed payments, memos, and invoices tracked separately for vendors and customers, plus expense tracking.

Reporting Dashboard

Business-level visibility across inventory, sales, and accounts.

Role-Based Access

Separate permission levels for admin, staff, and customer accounts, including a customer-facing catalog with per-customer pricing.

Camera-Based Tag Scanner

Scans jewelry tags carrying both a QR code and a CODE_128 barcode using the device camera, for fast stock lookup.

File Handling

Product image uploads and Excel-based bulk import for inventory/memo items.

Why I'm Building It

A friend of mine runs into this problem daily at his company — their data is scattered across spreadsheets and inventory software that was never built for how jewelry and diamonds actually get bought, sold, and tracked. Since I've spent years in this industry, at a diamond manufacturer and now at a jewelry wholesaler, I offered to build the system myself instead of pointing him toward another generic tool that wouldn't fit. That means owning the entire design from scratch — the database schema, the inventory logic, and how it connects to the B2B sales side — shaped around how jewelry businesses actually operate, not retrofitted from a generic inventory product.

My Role

I designed the data model and business workflows end-to-end — drawing on my SQL and database background to model inventory, vendor, and customer relationships — and directed the full-stack implementation using AI-assisted development.
